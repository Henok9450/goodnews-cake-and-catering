## 1. Compliance with Brand Identity Guardrails

A critical review identified that while a generic tiered cake icon communicated the business category, it introduced visual elements absent from the official brand logo. 

To achieve **100% brand unity**, the favicon was redesigned to directly isolate the signature cursive **"G"** letterform from the official GoodNews Cake & Catering logo (`logo3.png` / `logo1.png`).

## 2. Iconography Semantic Alignment

### Why the Authentic "G" Monogram?
1. **Direct Brand Identity:**
   Customers seeing the browser tab, mobile home screen bookmark, or PWA icon immediately connect it to the official "GoodNews" brand mark seen on the homepage header, packaging, and invoices.
2. **Standard Among Leading Consumer Brands:**
   Just as Netflix utilizes its red **"N"**, Pinterest utilizes its script **"P"**, and Facebook utilizes its lowercase **"f"**, GoodNews now possesses a proprietary, authentic **"G"** monogram.
3. **Multi-Tab Clarity:**
   The bold white "G" inside the ruby red squircle cuts through cluttered browser tabs with zero ambiguity, remaining pin-sharp even at 16×16 pixels.
