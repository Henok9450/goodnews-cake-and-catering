## 1. Executive Summary

On-page SEO optimization was implemented across all 9 public-facing pages of the GoodNews Cake & Catering application. Every page now features:
- A single, descriptive, primary `<h1>` heading containing natural search keywords and local modifiers (`Addis Ababa`).
- A dedicated, compelling introductory paragraph providing crawlable content for search engines without creating walls of text.
- Clear structural hierarchy (`<h1>` -> `<h2>` -> `<h3>`).
- Accurate, descriptive alternative text on all product photographs.

## 3. Product & Image Alt Text Optimization

Every product card and detail modal utilizes `<OptimizedImage>` with specific, descriptive `alt` attributes derived directly from verified product data:
- `Romantic Proposal Silhouette Cake`
- `Habesha Traditional Bridal & Milestone Cake`
- `Semi-Naked Cherry Chocolate Drip Cake`
- `Silver Pearl Grand Wedding Cake`
- `Golden Pearl Two-Tier Celebration Cake`
- `Spider-Man Superhero Celebration Cake`
- `Grand Continental Dessert & Pastry Banquet Table`
- `Executive Grand Banquet Buffet & Savory Table`
- `Enkutatash Ethiopian New Year Celebration Cake`

All image alt tags are distinct, avoiding generic placeholders like "cake photo" or "image 1".

## 4. Internal Linking Architecture

1. **Top Header Navigation:** Clean semantic links (`/gallery`, `/cupcakes`, `/cookies`, `/seasonal`, `/custom-order`, `/catering`, `/about`).
2. **Hero Action Banners:** Prominent dual calls to action (`/gallery` and `/custom-order`).
3. **Footer Linking:** Complete crawlable link clusters for quick navigation, legal, and local contact.
4. **Contextual Cross-Linking:** Deep link sharing buttons linking directly to specific product modals via URL parameters.
